# Turning gait and touch

[Download the encrypted dataset](analysis_data.7z)

The archive contains a CSV with 190 observations: 19 participants × 2 radii × 5 conditions.

**Password hint:** Enter the last two words of the revised manuscript title, in lowercase and without spaces.

Open the archive with 7-Zip or another compatible application.

Variables: `V` = velocity (m/s); `SW` = signed mean step width (mm); `ML` and `AP` = T5 angular RMS (degrees).

Codes: `Radius` 1 = 0.5 m, 2 = 1.0 m; `ConditionCode` 1 = NT, 2 = SV-LT, 3 = SV-FT, 4 = PV-LT, 5 = PV-FT.
